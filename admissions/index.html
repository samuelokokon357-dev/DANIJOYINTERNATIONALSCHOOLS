Admissions

Apply for a full boarding place at Danijoy International Schools, Uyo.

How admission works

  1. Send your enquiry

    Fill in the form below. It opens WhatsApp so the school office gets your details straight away.

  2. Take the entrance exam

    The office will confirm the next exam date and what to bring.

  3. Register your child

    Once your child is offered a place, the office guides you through registration and boarding needs.
