A school built on honesty and learning

Danijoy International Schools, Uyo Campus, is a full boarding secondary school in Ifiayong, Uruan L.G.A., Akwa Ibom State. Our students follow the Cambridge curriculum, take part in music, sport, art and public speaking, and live and study on a quiet campus.

Probitas et eruditio legatum estHonesty and learning is our legacy
  • Integrity. We teach students to be honest in their work and in their words.
  • Excellence. We expect strong results and we celebrate them.
  • Leadership. Students lead, speak and mentor others.
  • Creativity. Music, dance and art are part of the school week.

What every Danijoy student receives

Six commitments that shape the school day, from the first lesson to lights out in the boarding house.

Value-based, holistic education

Integrity sits beside grades. Students are taught to do the right thing when no one is watching.

Creative and vocational training

Music, art, dance and practical skills sit alongside the core subjects.

International certifications

Cambridge qualifications, with preparation for IELTS and SAT.

Leadership and mentorship

Students take on responsibility and are guided by teachers who know them by name.

World-class learning and boarding

Science laboratories, a computer room, interactive boards and comfortable dormitories.

A serene environment

A calm campus away from the noise of the city, made for focus.

Our people

Teachers and staff who guide students in class and in the boarding house.

Academics

We are a Cambridge school. Students work towards internationally recognised qualifications and prepare for the IELTS and SAT.

Sciences

Hands-on practicals in our laboratory, from chemistry to physics and biology.

Core subjects

English, mathematics and the subjects every student needs, taught by qualified teachers.

Assessment

Regular tests and exams show every student where they stand and what to work on next.

Rooms made for learning

  • Bright, ordered classrooms with proper desks
  • A science laboratory for practical lessons
  • A computer room
  • Interactive boards for visual teaching
  • Boarding houses for rest and prep

Student life

Music, sport, art, public speaking and broadcasting. There's a place for every talent.

Live on the radio

Students visit a local radio studio to present, interview and speak live on air.

Culture on stage

Our dance troupe performs traditional dances in hand-made costume, with painted faces, beads and ankle rattles. They perform at school events and bring local culture into school life.

Proud moments

Our students were recognised at the 2025/2026 end-of-session awards and at the National Mathematics Festival.
